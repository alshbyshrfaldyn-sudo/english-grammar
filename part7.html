Unit 19: I have done vs I did (Present Perfect vs Past Simple)
I have lost my key. I lost it yesterday.
الترجمة: لقد أضعت مفتاحي (أثر في الحاضر). أضعته بالأمس (تحديد زمني في الماضي البسيط).
Did you see Kate this morning?
الترجمة: هل رأيت كيت هذا الصباح؟ (استخدام Past Simple لأن فترة الصباح انتهت).
Tense Time Concept Keywords
Present Perfect Unfinished time / Connection to now today, this week, recently, ever
Past Simple Finished time in the past yesterday, last week, in 2015, ago
Unit 20: is done / was done (Passive 1 - Present and Past)
This office is cleaned every day.
الترجمة: يتم تنظيف هذا المكتب كل يوم. (المبني للمجهول في المضارع البسيط: is/are + V3).
The house was built in 1990.
الترجمة: بُني المنزل في عام 1990. (المبني للمجهول في الماضي البسيط: was/were + V3).
Tense Passive Structure Example
Present Simple Passive am / is / are + Past Participle Butter is made from milk.
Past Simple Passive was / were + Past Participle Two rooms were painted.
Unit 21: is being done / has been done (Passive 2)
My car is being repaired at the moment.
الترجمة: سيارتي قيد الإصلاح حالياً. (المبني للمجهول في المضارع المستمر: is/are + being + V3).
The room has been cleaned.
الترجمة: تم تنظيف الغرفة. (المبني للمجهول في المضارع التام: has/have + been + V3).
Tense Passive Structure Example
Present Continuous Passive is / are + being + Past Participle A new road is being built.
Present Perfect Passive has / have + been + Past Participle The key has been found.
