Unit 112: More Adjectives + Prepositions (proud of, famous for, etc.)
He is proud of his academic achievements.
الترجمة: إنه فخور بإنجازاته الأكاديمية. (proud يأتي معها حرف الجر of).
Yemen is famous for its coffee and history.
الترجمة: اليمن مشهور بقهوته وتاريخه. (famous يأتي معها حرف الجر for).
Adjective + Preposition Meaning Example
proud of فخور بـ They are proud of their team.
famous for مشهور بـ The city is famous for its architecture.
full of مليء بـ The room was full of people.
Unit 113: Verbs + Prepositions Part 2 (depend on, belong to, etc.)
It depends on the weather conditions.
الترجمة: يعتمد ذلك على الظروف الجوية. (depend يأتي معها on).
Does this book belong to you?
الترجمة: هل ينتمي هذا الكتاب إليك؟ (belong يأتي معها to).
Verb + Preposition Meaning Example
depend on يعتمد على Our plans depend on the weather.
belong to ينتمي إلى / يخص This car belongs to my brother.
think about / of يفكر في / يعطي رأياً بـ What do you think of this idea?
Unit 114: Complex Prepositions (because of, instead of, in front of)
We stayed indoors because of the heavy rain.
الترجمة: بقينا في الداخل بسبب المطر الغزير. (because of يأتي بعدها اسم أو اسم مجرور).
He took tea instead of coffee.
الترجمة: أخذ شاي بدلاً من القهوة.
Compound Preposition Meaning Example Sentence
because of بسبب The flight was delayed because of fog.
instead of بدلاً من I will use Python instead of PHP.
in front of / behind أمام / خلف Park your car in front of the house.
