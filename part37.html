Unit 109: go to..., go for..., go -ing
Let's go for a walk or go swimming.
الترجمة: دعنا نذهب للمشي أو نذهب للسباحة. (go for + اسم للأنشطة، go + verb-ing للرياضات والأنشطة).
They go shopping every weekend.
الترجمة: هم يذهبون للتسوق كل نهاية أسبوع.
Structure Usage Examples
go to + place الانتقال إلى مكان go to London, go to sleep, go to work
go for + noun الذهاب لغرض نشاط محدد go for a walk, go for a drink, go for a run
go + Verb-ing ممارسة الأنشطة والرياضات go swimming, go shopping, go skiing
Unit 110: Expressions with get and do/make
I usually get to work at 8:30 AM.
الترجمة: أصل عادة إلى العمل في الساعة 8:30 صباحاً. (get to تعني يصل إلى).
I need to make a phone call before I do my homework.
الترجمة: أحتاج إلى إجراء مكالمة هاتفية قبل أن أعمل واجبي. (make للصناعة/الإنشاء، do للأنشطة والواجبات).
Verb Collocations / Expressions Example
get get married, get tired, get lost, get to (arrive) Did you get lost on your way?
do do homework, do business, do the laundry, do research She is doing research in AI.
make make a mistake, make a call, make a decision, make coffee Don't worry if you make a mistake.
Unit 111: Expressions with have (have breakfast, have a look, etc.)
We had a great time at the conference.
الترجمة: قضينا وقتاً رائعاً في المؤتمر. (have تعني يقضي/يتناول هنا، ولا تُختصر بـ 've في هذه التراكيب).
Can I have a look at your project?
الترجمة: هل يمكنني إلقاء نظرة على مشروعك؟
Category Expressions with have Example
Meals & Drinks have breakfast, have lunch, have dinner, have a cup of tea I had lunch at 1:00.
Actions & Experiences have a shower, have a rest, have a look, have a holiday I'll have a shower now.
Social Interactions have a conversation, have a chat, have a party We had a chat about the results.
